Eco-Friendly Destinations

Discover sustainable travel spots across Kenya

Kakamega Forest National Reserve

Western Kenya · Forest Region

Kenya's only true tropical rainforest, home to rare birds, primates, and guided canopy nature walks.

Watamu Marine National Park

Coast Region

A protected coral reef and marine park offering snorkeling tours and sea turtle conservation visits.

Aberdare National Park

Central Highlands Region

Misty highland forests and moorlands with waterfalls, guided hikes, and rare bongo antelope sightings.

Lake Naivasha

Rift Valley · Lakes Region

A freshwater lake offering boat safaris, hippo viewing, and birdwatching with a strong no-motor conservation zone.

Karura Forest

Nairobi · Forest Region

An urban forest sanctuary in Nairobi with cycling trails, waterfalls, and community-led reforestation projects.

Diani Beach Mangrove Boardwalk

Coast Region

Raised boardwalks through restored mangrove forests, supporting coastal erosion control and local fisheries.

Mount Kenya National Park

Highlands Region

A UNESCO World Heritage Site offering guided treks, alpine lakes, and community-run mountain lodges.

Lake Nakuru National Park

Rift Valley · Lakes Region

A soda lake famous for flamingo flocks and rhino sanctuary tours run with local conservancy partners.
