About GreenTrails

Connecting travelers with Kenya's most sustainable destinations

Our Mission

GreenTrails was founded to make eco-conscious travel easier to plan and more accessible to everyday travelers. We believe tourism can be a powerful force for conservation when it directly benefits the communities and ecosystems that make these destinations worth visiting in the first place.

Every destination, lodge, and itinerary featured on this platform is selected because of a verified commitment to sustainability — whether that's solar power, community land-lease agreements, or active conservation funding.

What "Eco-Friendly" Means to Us
  • Renewable energy use (solar, wind, micro-grid)
  • Direct revenue sharing with local communities
  • Low-impact wildlife viewing practices
  • Water conservation and waste reduction
  • Support for habitat and species conservation

Our Core Values

Conservation First

We only list destinations and stays with a genuine, verifiable conservation impact.

Community Partnership

We prioritize community-owned conservancies and locally led tourism initiatives.

Honest Information

No greenwashing. We verify every sustainability claim before featuring a listing.
