Travel Itineraries

Ready-made day-by-day plans for a more sustainable adventure

3-Day Masai Mara Eco-Safari

A low-impact safari experience focused on community conservancies rather than crowded reserve routes.

Arrive at Ol Kinyei Conservancy and check in at a community-run eco-camp. In the afternoon, take a low-vehicle-density game drive guided by a local Maasai ranger, with proceeds supporting conservancy land lease payments to local landowners.

Morning guided walking safari to track wildlife on foot with minimal disturbance. In the afternoon, visit a local Maasai village for a cultural exchange that directly supports the homestead through a fair-trade visitor fee.

An early sunrise game drive followed by breakfast at camp. Check out and depart, with an optional stop at a local women's beadwork cooperative on the way out.

4-Day Amboseli & Chyulu Hills Trail

Combine elephant viewing beneath Kilimanjaro with highland forest hikes in the Chyulu Hills.

Arrive at Amboseli National Park and settle into a solar-powered tented camp. Evening game drive with views of Mount Kilimanjaro and resident elephant herds.

Full-day game drive across the park's swamps and plains, followed by a visit to the Amboseli Elephant Research Centre to learn about long-term conservation tracking.

Morning transfer to the Chyulu Hills. Afternoon guided hike through volcanic highland forest with views over the rift valley, staying at a community-partnered lodge.

Visit the Leviathan lava tube caves with a local guide before departing in the early afternoon.

3-Day Watamu & Diani Coastal Escape

A marine-focused trip combining reef conservation with mangrove restoration walks.

Arrive in Watamu and join a guided snorkeling trip through the marine park's protected coral reef, run by a local conservation cooperative.

Visit a sea turtle rescue and release programme in the morning. In the afternoon, walk the Mida Creek mangrove boardwalk with a community ranger explaining restoration efforts.

Transfer to Diani Beach for a final morning walk along the mangrove boardwalk before departure.
